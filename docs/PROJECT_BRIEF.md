# FlowPilot project brief

FlowPilot is a hackathon prototype for small-business financial operations. Its working tagline is “Everything your business needs to flow.” The initial customer niche remains undecided; product language should stay relevant to small businesses generally.

## Current scope

The repository contains a public marketing homepage and a Supabase-backed private workspace prototype. The workspace includes authentication/onboarding, business summaries, customer and invoice records, payment/reminder interfaces, expense and bill records, cash-flow views, reports, and settings. See the root README for setup and current verification limits.

The product must distinguish recorded values from projections and illustrative homepage examples. Reminder copy is not sent externally. Financial calculations are deterministic and should use saved business data.

## Future product vision

Potential future work includes a complete password recovery flow, reliable payment recording through the public API, deeper authenticated workflow coverage, financial alerts, expanded exports, and integrations. Do not imply these are fully operational unless implemented and verified.

## Hackathon context

ACE Builders Hackathon 2026, Problem Statement 1 — “The One-Person Back Office.” The intended workflows include invoice-to-payment tracking, drafted follow-ups, receivables visibility, cash forecasts, and alerts with next actions.
